# Sources — SOC 2200

Two kinds of citation appear in this course, and they are kept apart on purpose: **books and articles**
(author, title, year — no links) and **the shows' record** (the reference site, which is the only
external link this course uses).

## Books and articles

### Core readings

- OpenStax, *Introduction to Sociology 3e* (Rice University, 2021), ch. 11 — the survey-level anchor
  for race and ethnicity.
- Michael Omi and Howard Winant, *Racial Formation in the United States* (3rd ed., 2014) — the course's
  central theory, used from Module 1 through the capstone.

### Concept bibliography

- W. E. B. Du Bois, *The Souls of Black Folk* (1903) — the color line.
- Gordon Allport, *The Nature of Prejudice* (1954) — prejudice as ordinary cognition.
- Erving Goffman, *Stigma: Notes on the Management of Spoiled Identity* (1963) — stigma.
- Milton Gordon, *Assimilation in American Life* (1964) — assimilation.
- Daniel Patrick Moynihan, *The Negro Family: The Case for National Action* (1965) — the Moynihan debate.
- Stuart Hall, "Encoding and Decoding in the Television Discourse" (1973) — encoding/decoding.
- Kimberlé Crenshaw, "Demarginalizing the Intersection of Race and Sex" (1989) — intersectionality.
- Patricia Hill Collins, *Black Feminist Thought* (1990) — Black feminist thought; othermothering.
- Evelyn Brooks Higginbotham, *Righteous Discontent* (1993) — the politics of respectability.
- bell hooks, *Black Looks: Race and Representation* (1992) — the oppositional gaze.
- Melvin Oliver and Thomas Shapiro, *Black Wealth / White Wealth* (1995) — the racial wealth gap.
- Stuart Hall, ed., *Representation: Cultural Representations and Signifying Practices* (1997) —
  representation.
- Richard Alba and Victor Nee, *Remaking the American Mainstream* (2003) — assimilation, revised.
- David Theo Goldberg, *The Racial State* (2002) — the racial state.
- Margaret Hunter, *Race, Gender, and the Politics of Skin Tone* (2005) — colourism.
- Michael Burawoy, "For Public Sociology" (2005) — public sociology.
- Michelle Alexander, *The New Jim Crow* (2010) — mass incarceration.
- Eduardo Bonilla-Silva, *Racism without Racists* (2021) — colour-blind racism and structural racism.

### Vocabulary reference (cited by the course, owned by the reference project)

- Geneva Smitherman, *Black Talk* (1994) · Clarence Major, *Juba to Jive* (1994) · Rickford & Rickford,
  *Spoken Soul* (2000) · the *Oxford English Dictionary* · Green's *Dictionary of Slang*.

## Attribution and use

*A Different World* (1987–1993 and 2026) is the property of its rights holders; this course reproduces
no scripts, stills, or footage, and uses episode titles, air dates, and published summaries only for
identification and commentary. Statistics and data cited in the course are the property of their
sources and are flagged as "check before you teach" items rather than fixed constants.
