# Homework 6 — One standard, inside a group

**Due:** start of Module 7 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Choose one *standard* that ranks people *inside* a group (skin tone, accent, presentation, class
   markers, "respectability") and show how it works: who sets it, who enforces it, and who pays for
   falling short. Use the Miss Gilbert Hall pageant (the second half of Ep 7) as at least one example.
2. Using Higginbotham's **politics of respectability**, explain in two or three sentences what the
   standard *promises* the people who meet it — and what it costs the people it ranks below.

## Sources

- The reference site (the Miss Gilbert Hall and Darlene Duvall cards): https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/
- Higginbotham, *Righteous Discontent* (1993), introduction (from the module).
