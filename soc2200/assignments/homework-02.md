# Homework 2 — Build a racial project

**Due:** start of Module 3 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Using Omi and Winant's definition, build one **racial project** of your own: a specific
   interpretation or representation of a racial category, linked to a specific institutional
   arrangement. State (a) what the category *means* in your project, and (b) how that meaning is
   *organised* into practice. It may be drawn from the screened talent-show argument or invented, but it
   must be concrete, not generic.
2. In two or three sentences, use your project to explain what the first-half talent-show argument in Ep
   7 is *doing* sociologically — which meaning it is linking to which arrangement.
