# Homework 12 — Who holds it together

**Due:** start of Module 13 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Take one family network you know (your own, or a community you can observe) and map the **care
   work**: who actually does the feeding, the checking-in, the crisis-absorbing, the passing-on of
   obligations. Use **othermothering** and **kinship** to name what a nuclear-family lens would miss.
2. In two or three sentences, use the aunties' arrival in *Insomnia and the Daydreamer* (the screened
   scene) to make one claim about how race shapes family — and say whether the Moynihan debate's framing
   would see that claim as a deficit or a resource, and why.
