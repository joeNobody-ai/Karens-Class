# Homework 13 — One mechanism of criminalization

**Due:** start of Module 14 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Pick **one** mechanism by which a population or behaviour is *made criminal* (a law, a policing
   practice, a sentencing rule, a "war on drugs" policy, a bail schedule) and trace it: what does it
   define as criminal, and who does it sweep in? Keep the mechanism concrete and named.
2. Using Alexander's argument, explain in two or three sentences how this single mechanism could
   operate as part of a *system* of control even if no one running it is individually racist. Point to
   the mechanism's cumulative effect, not anyone's heart.

- Alexander, *The New Jim Crow* (2010), ch. 1 (from the module).
