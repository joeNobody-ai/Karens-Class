# Homework 15 — The capstone brief

**Due:** final session (Module 15) · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

This is the brief for your capstone. Bring it to the final session ready to defend.

1. State **one plain claim** about race that the finale *A Gift & The Curse* (the screened scene) best
   supports — arguable, specific, and unmistakably yours. One sentence.
2. Name the **two concepts** that do the most distinct work on that claim, and say, in one sentence
   each, what each concept reveals that the other cannot. Then name **one honest limit**: what the scene
   cannot show, that you would need another source to establish.
