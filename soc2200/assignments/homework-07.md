# Homework 7 — The wealth gap, by the numbers

**Due:** start of Module 8 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

This is the **data exercise**. Find a real, primary figure about the racial wealth gap and use it
honestly.

1. Find **one** primary statistic about the racial wealth gap (a median wealth ratio, a median
   household wealth figure by race, a homeownership-rate gap). State: (a) the figure, (b) the source
   and its year, and (c) the denominator or comparison group the figure is measured against. A figure
   without a denominator or comparison group is incomplete.
2. In two or three sentences, use the figure to make **one** careful claim about race and class — and
   name one thing the figure cannot show (e.g. it is a snapshot, a median, a correlation, not a causal
   story).
