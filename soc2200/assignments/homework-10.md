# Homework 10 — What an institution does

**Due:** start of Module 11 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Choose one institution (an HBCU, a school, a church, a hospital, a union) and name its **function** —
   what it actually *does* for a group that a different institution could not simply replace. Be
   concrete: name the people, the resources, and the arrangements.
2. Using the Ep 9 budget crisis (the screened scene) as your test case, explain in two or three
   sentences what is lost if the institution shrinks — and why "a generic campus could do the same job"
   is or is not a fair claim in that case.
