# Homework 11 — One disparity, by the numbers

**Due:** start of Module 12 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

This is a **data exercise**, like Homework 7 but on health. Find a real, primary figure and use it
honestly.

1. Find **one** primary statistic about a racial health disparity (a mortality-rate gap, a maternal
   mortality figure, an incidence or life-expectancy gap). State: (a) the figure, (b) the source and
   its year, and (c) the denominator or comparison group. No denominator, no credit for completeness.
2. In two or three sentences, use the figure to make **one** careful claim about why health follows the
   color line — and name **one social determinant** the figure points to but does not itself measure.
