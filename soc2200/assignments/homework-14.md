# Homework 14 — A ruled-on boundary

**Due:** start of Module 15 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Take one boundary of Blackness (or of another identity you can observe closely) that is actively
   *ruled on* — who is in, who is out, who decides. Using **category work**, name who holds the
   authority to rule, what gives them that authority, and what a person gains or loses by the ruling.
2. In two or three sentences, read the full Ep 7 (the screened episode) through your boundary: what
   does your example make you see in the episode's argument about belonging — and what does the episode
   make you see in your example? Mark this as your reading.
