# Homework 3 — Feelings, acts, and systems

**Due:** start of Module 4 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Take one real-world case you have read or heard about this term (a news item, a policy, an incident)
   and sort it into the three columns from Module 3: **prejudice** (a feeling), **discrimination** (an
   act), and **institutional racism** (a system). Give one concrete instance for each column, or say
   explicitly that one column is empty and why.
2. In two or three sentences, explain which of the three the case is *mainly* about — and what evidence
   you would need to be sure. Be honest about the limit of what you currently know.
