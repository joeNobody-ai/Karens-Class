# Homework 4 — Find the state in a policy

**Due:** start of Module 5 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Pick one real policy — a budget line, a zoning rule, an admissions or lending criterion, a bail or
   sentencing guideline — and show where the *state* is doing racial work even though the word "race"
   never appears. Name the policy, and trace who it sorts and how.
2. Using the concept of the **racial state** (Goldberg), explain in two or three sentences why the
   policy's colour-blind language is not evidence of colour-blind effects. Point to the *mechanism*, not
   the intent.

## Sources

- The reference site (the budget-crisis card): https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/
- Goldberg, *The Racial State* (2002), introduction (from the module).
