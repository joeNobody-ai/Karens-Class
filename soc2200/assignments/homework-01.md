# Homework 1 — Invented, but not imaginary

**Due:** start of Module 2 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Pick one category that sorts people (race, or another category like "nerd," "foreigner," or
   "townie") and, in your own words, walk it through the construction loop from Module 1: it is made,
   enforced, internalised, and comes to look natural. Use the *Irreconcilable Differences* /
   *Reconcilable Differences* echo from the screened scene as at least one of your examples.
2. Then answer the big question in two or three sentences of your own: if the category is invented, why
   are its consequences real? Point to one concrete consequence, not a general feeling.
