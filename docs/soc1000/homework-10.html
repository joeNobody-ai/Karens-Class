Homework 10 — Personal or structural?

Due: start of Session 11 · Length: 1 page · Weight: counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

Task

  1. Take one relationship — from the week's material (Ep 4's "cuffing season", the parents' marriage) or from life — and ask the course's standing question: is this personal, structural, or both?
  2. Answer in a compact block: - The relationship: who it is between, in one sentence. - What is personal: the part that is about the two people themselves. - What is structural: the part that was shaped before they met (class, geography, education, the "marriage market", family form). - Your judgment: which matters more here, and what evidence you would need to be sure.

Sources

  • Session 10 (family forms, kinship, mate selection, the marriage market, othermothering)
  • The reference site: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/

Criteria (out of 10)

Points For
3 The personal and structural parts are actually separated, not blurred
3 The structural part names a real mechanism (not just "society")
2 The judgment is argued with a testable standard
2 Written with respect for the people involved
