Homework 4 — An agent of your own socialization

Due: start of Session 5 · Length: 1 page · Weight: counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

Task

  1. Name one agent of socialization in your own life (a person, an institution, a peer group, a piece of media) that taught you a norm or a role you now follow.
  2. Answer in a compact block: - The agent: who or what it was. - What it taught: the specific norm, value, or role. - How: the mechanism — instruction, example, reward, correction, imitation. - Was it anticipatory? Were you learning for a role you did not yet hold? Say why or why not.
  3. One sentence on how Session 4's "aunties" example compares to your own case.

Sources

  • Session 4 (socialization, agents, anticipatory socialization, othermothering)
  • The lingo reference: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/lingo.html

Criteria (out of 10)

Points For
3 The agent and what it taught are specific, not generic
3 The mechanism (how it taught) is identified
2 The anticipatory-socialization question is answered with a reason
2 The comparison to the aunties is real, not decorative
