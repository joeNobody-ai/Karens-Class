Homework 9 — A script and its break

Due: start of Session 10 · Length: 1 page · Weight: counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

Task

  1. Identify one gender script — the expected way a man or a woman should behave in a scene (a date, a pageant, a breakup, a workplace, a classroom).
  2. Use a scene from the week's material, or one from your own observation.
  3. Answer in a compact block: - The script: what the expected performance is. - Who enforces it: who holds the performer to the standard, and how. - The break: a moment where someone deviates from the script — what happens when they do. - Intersection: one way race or class changes how this script plays out.

Sources

  • Session 9 (doing gender, gender scripts, gender stratification, intersectionality)
  • The lingo reference: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/lingo.html

Criteria (out of 10)

Points For
3 The script is stated as a shared expectation, not a personal gripe
3 The enforcement and the break are both named
2 The intersection (race/class shaping the script) is real, not an afterthought
2 Specific scene or example, in your own words
