Homework 6 — A label and its cost

Due: start of Session 7 · Length: 1 page · Weight: counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

Task

  1. Choose one label that gets applied to a person or a group (a stigma, a "deviant" label, a master status) — from the week's material or from the world around you.
  2. Answer in a compact block: - The label: what it is, stated plainly. - Who applies it: who has the power to make it stick. - The cost: what the labelled person loses or carries because of it (standing, access, safety, a future). - Medical or moral? Is the label treated as a medical condition or a moral failing — and what difference does that make?

Sources

  • Session 6 (deviance, labeling theory, stigma, social control, medicalization)
  • The reference site, for the week's documented material: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/

Criteria (out of 10)

Points For
3 The label and who applies it are specific
3 The cost is named concretely, not just "it is bad"
2 The medical/moral distinction is argued with a consequence
2 The discussion stays respectful of the people labelled (the material is about labels, not about mocking anyone)
