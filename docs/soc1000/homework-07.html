Homework 7 — Find the number

Due: start of Session 8 · Length: 1 page · Weight: counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

Task

This is a data exercise: go get one real number and report it honestly.

  1. Pick one claim about inequality that Session 7 raised — for example: the wealth gap, student debt, college costs, or who graduates with what.
  2. Find one primary figure (from a government agency, a research institute, or an academic source — not a news article about a study).
  3. Report it in a compact block: - The figure: the number, exactly as the source states it. - The source: who published it, and the year. - The denominator or comparison: what it is a fraction or comparison of. - One honest limit: one thing this number cannot tell you.

Sources

  • Start at the reference site's sources page and the course's sources page, then go to the primary source.
  • Reference site: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/

Criteria (out of 10)

Points For
3 The figure is from a primary source and quoted correctly
3 The denominator or comparison group is stated (no naked numbers)
2 The limit is about measurement, not a shrug ("it might be wrong")
2 Source and year given, URL included
