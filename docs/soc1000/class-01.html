Session 1 — The Sociological Imagination

Big question. What does it mean to see your own life as history?

Where we start. With two screens showing the same story 39 years apart. Sociology is not a list of facts about society; it is a way of looking — the habit of connecting a private trouble to a public issue. We start with the show because it makes that connection visible in ninety seconds.

Before class: viewing

Text Episode First aired Why this one
Revival (2026) Ep 1, Irreconcilable Differences 2026-09-24 Deborah Wayne arrives at Hillman, following her parents
Original (1987) S1E1, Reconcilable Differences 1987-09-24 Denise Huxtable arrives at the same campus, 39 years earlier
Optional clip The two openings, back to back — The whole course in one pairing

Reference site for both, with sources: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/

Sociological frame

  1. The sociological imagination (C. Wright Mills, 1959): the capacity to see "personal troubles" as "public issues". A first-year student who cannot afford to stay is a private problem — until you ask how many first-years cannot afford to stay, and why.
  2. Structure and agency: the course's standing tension. Structure is what we are born into (an institution, a family reputation, an economy); agency is what we choose inside it. The show is built on this tension.
  3. Biography and history: Mills's two halves. Every biography — Deborah's, yours — unfolds inside a history the person did not choose.
  4. The social versus the personal: the difference this course will keep returning to. Something is social when it would be true even if a different person were standing in the same place.

The 75 minutes

Min What
0–8 The pairing. Screen the two openings back to back, no comment. Ask only: what did you notice? Write answers verbatim on the board — this list is the course's raw material
8–20 What sociology is. Introduce Mills and the sociological imagination. Define the four terms above in plain words, tied to what is already on the board
20–33 Structure and agency through Deborah. What is chosen, what is inherited, what is both. The "39 years to the day" fact (both premieres on 24 September) as biography meeting history
33–48 The evidence rule. Documented / interpretation / hypothesis, worked on one callback: the title echo (Reconcilable vs Irreconcilable)
48–62 Pair drill. Each pair takes one callback card from the reference site and sorts it into the three tiers; they must be able to say what would change their mind
62–75 The course. Schedule, assessment, norms. Assign homework 1. Name the content policy out loud

Discussion questions

  1. What is a college — a setting, a character, or an argument — in each opening?
  2. Deborah is the daughter of the original's central couple. What does the revival gain and lose by making its lead an inheritance rather than an arrival?
  3. Name one thing in your own life that felt personal but that you could also describe as a public issue.
  4. The two shows premiered on the same date 39 years apart. Is that evidence of intent, or scheduling? What would settle it?
  5. What would a sociologist need that a television scene cannot give?

Instructor notes

If the room says "it's just a sitcom." Take it seriously and give it teeth: ask what television has historically done to public knowledge — who learned what about which places from which shows. If the only fictional historically Black college most Americans can name came from a 1987 sitcom, that is itself a finding about how knowledge is distributed.

If a student says "my life is nothing like this." That is data about portrayal, not a refutation. Ask what the show would have to show for them to recognise it — and hold the difference between a sample of one and a claim about a population.

If someone asks about the show's origins. The original began as a Cosby Show spinoff, and one of its originators was later convicted of sexual offences. Do not resolve this; name it and note the course will return to it in Module 12 when it takes up image and representation directly. The 2026 revival was made by a largely new creative team with several original cast members returning.

Watch for. The student who already knows the franchise is an asset; give them the job of correcting the room with sources, using the reference site.
