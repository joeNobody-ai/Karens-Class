Homework 14 — Figure, source, denominator

Due: start of Session 15 · Length: 2 pages · Weight: counts in the homework average

Task

The state, the street, and the ledger, in numbers you found yourself.

  1. Wealth and income, side by side. Find two current, citable figures from primary sources (federal statistical agencies, the Federal Reserve's Survey of Consumer Finances, peer-reviewed studies): - median household wealth by race, and - median household income by race, for the most recent year available. Report each with source, table or report name, URL, retrieval date, and the comparison being made.
  2. The mechanism. In 400 words, explain how income gaps and wealth gaps can move in opposite directions. Name mechanisms: inheritance, home equity and its histories, credit access, pensions, student and consumer debt, business capital, incarceration's economic effects. Cite at least two sources.
  3. The 1992 text. Original S6E120 (Honeymoon in L.A. (part 1), 1992-09-24) opens a season with a police-brutality trial and the uprising that followed. In 300 words: describe what the episode does with that material, and what it assumes about its audience. Facts only — use the episode's documented summary and, if you want context, cite one historical source on the 1992 Los Angeles uprising.
  4. Where is that tradition now? In 2026 Episode 9, students organise against an institutional crisis. In 250 words, compare what each text asks its audience to do with what they have seen.
  5. One sentence. State the strongest argument that the revival's organising plot is a different kind of political storytelling than the 1992 premiere — and then say whether you believe it.

Sources

  • Ep 9 callback cards, including the Rodney King precedent: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/ep09.html#ep09-rodney-king-precedent
  • Michelle Alexander, The New Jim Crow (2010), introduction
  • Richard Rothstein, The Color of Law (2017), introduction
  • Isabel Wilkerson, The Warmth of Other Suns (2010)
  • Primary-source starting points (HTTP-checked at build; verify before use): the Federal Reserve's Survey of Consumer Finances (https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/scfindex.htm), the Census Bureau's race data (https://www.census.gov/topics/population/race.html) and wealth data (https://www.census.gov/topics/income-poverty/wealth.html), and the Bureau of Justice Statistics (https://bjs.ojp.gov/)

Criteria (out of 10)

Points For
3 Two figures, primary sources, correct years and comparison groups
3 The mechanism argument is causal and sourced, not moralised
2 The 1992 analysis uses documented facts and states its assumptions about audience
2 The final sentence states the strongest opposing case before your own answer

Integrity note: if a figure is contested or has multiple versions (definitions differ!), say so and use one consistently. Reporting a range honestly beats reporting a point estimate you cannot defend.
