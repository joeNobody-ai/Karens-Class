Homework 7 — Two accounts of one category

Due: start of Session 8 · Length: 2 pages · Weight: counts in the homework average

Task

2026 Episode 7 stages an argument about African and diaspora identity. Your job is to reconstruct that argument as fairly as possible, and then adjudicate it as evidence.

  1. Reconstruct account A (roughly, Kojo's position as the episode presents it) in 200 words. It must be an argument a reasonable person could hold, stated in its strongest form.
  2. Reconstruct account B (the response from the American-born students) in 200 words, same standard.
  3. What is the disagreement actually about? Choose one: ancestry, culture, nationality, class, or experience — and defend your choice with a specific moment or line of argument from the episode.
  4. The 1990 comparison. In A World Alike (S3E60), an anti-apartheid boycott would cost a Black student her scholarship. Describe how that episode allocates cost, and what it assumes about its audience's political literacy.
  5. One paragraph of adjudication. Which account does the revival favour, and what is your evidence? If the episode refuses to choose, that refusal is itself an argument — say what it does.

Sources

  • Ep 7 callback cards: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/ep07.html
  • Original: S3E60 A World Alike (1990-02-15)
  • Michael Omi and Howard Winant, Racial Formation in the United States (3rd ed., 2014) — chapters on racial formation as a process
  • W. E. B. Du Bois, The Souls of Black Folk (1903) — for double consciousness and the colour line

Criteria (out of 10)

Points For
4 Both accounts are stated in their strongest form, without caricature
3 The disagreement is located precisely, with evidence from the text
2 The 1990 comparison is accurate about the dilemma and its cost
1 The adjudication distinguishes what the episode does from what you wish it did

Warning: this assignment is graded on fairness of reconstruction. A submission that makes one side obviously foolish will lose most of the marks even if your own view is correct.
