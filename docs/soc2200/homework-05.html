Homework 5 — Map a migration

Due: start of Module 6 · Length: 1 page · Weight: counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

Task

  1. Choose one migration story — your own family, a group you have read about, or a community you know — and map it with three of the module's concepts: immigration, assimilation, and diaspora (or racialized nativism). For each, one sentence on what the concept reveals about that story.
  2. Then read the Ep 7 diaspora argument (the screened scene) through your mapped story: what does your story make you see in the scene that you missed on first viewing? One paragraph, marked as your reading.

Sources

  • The reference site (the diaspora-identity and talent-show cards): https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/
  • Alba and Nee, Remaking the American Mainstream (2003), ch. 1 (from the module).

Criteria (out of 10)

Points For
4 All three concepts do distinct work on one concrete migration story
3 The scene is read through the story, producing a new observation, not a summary
2 The reading is labelled as yours and kept apart from documented facts
1 One page, plain prose, no padding
