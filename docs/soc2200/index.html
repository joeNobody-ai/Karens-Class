The 15 modules

Module 1

Race as a Social Construction

If race is not biological, why are its consequences so real?

Module 2

Race, Ethnicity, and Racial Formation

How is a racial category made — and remade — over time?

Module 3

Prejudice, Discrimination, and Racism

What is the difference between what people feel and what institutions do?

Module 4

Racial Formation and the State

How does the state make race through law and policy?

Module 5

Immigration and the Racial Order

How does migration remake the boundaries of race and belonging?

Module 6

Colourism and Intraracial Stratification

How does a hierarchy form inside a group that is itself stratified against?

Module 7

Race and Class

Where do race and class meet — and where do they come apart?

Module 8

Race and Gender

Why does race change how gender is done — and vice versa?

Module 9

Race and Media Representation

Who controls the image of a race — and what does the image do?

Module 10

Race and Education: The HBCU

What does a historically Black college do that a generic campus cannot?

Module 11

Race and Health

Why does health follow the color line — even when access is equal?

Module 12

Race and Family

Module 13

Race, Crime, and Justice

Module 14

Racial Identity and Belonging

Module 15

Capstone: Racial Formation, Applied

Can you hold the whole course against one case and argue it?

How to use this site

Students: start with the syllabus, then Module 1. Homework is due at the start of the following module and each assignment says exactly what to hand in and how it is marked.

Instructors: read the instructor guide before Module 1. Exam keys are in this repository under soc2200/exams/ and, if built, at instructor/index.html — deliberately not linked from the navigation.

Evidence rule

The whole course runs on keeping three things apart: documented facts (episode titles, dates, credits, published summaries), interpretations (readings of what a scene or season argues), and hypotheses (claims on the table for testing). Students are graded on it from week one. The reference site does the same thing, which is why homework points there.
