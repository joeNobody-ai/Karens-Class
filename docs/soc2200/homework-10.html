Homework 10 — What an institution does

Due: start of Module 11 · Length: 1 page · Weight: counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

Task

  1. Choose one institution (an HBCU, a school, a church, a hospital, a union) and name its function — what it actually does for a group that a different institution could not simply replace. Be concrete: name the people, the resources, and the arrangements.
  2. Using the Ep 9 budget crisis (the screened scene) as your test case, explain in two or three sentences what is lost if the institution shrinks — and why "a generic campus could do the same job" is or is not a fair claim in that case.

Sources

  • The reference site (the student-organizing and Hillman College cards): https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/
  • OpenStax, Introduction to Sociology 3e (2021), ch. 16 (from the module).

Criteria (out of 10)

Points For
4 A real institution's function is named concretely, with people and arrangements
3 The budget crisis is used to test the "generic replacement" claim, for and against
2 The distinction between an institution's documented facts and your reading of its function is kept visible
1 One page, plain prose, no padding
