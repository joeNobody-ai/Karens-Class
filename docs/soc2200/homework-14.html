Homework 14 — A ruled-on boundary

Due: start of Module 15 · Length: 1 page · Weight: counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

Task

  1. Take one boundary of Blackness (or of another identity you can observe closely) that is actively ruled on — who is in, who is out, who decides. Using category work, name who holds the authority to rule, what gives them that authority, and what a person gains or loses by the ruling.
  2. In two or three sentences, read the full Ep 7 (the screened episode) through your boundary: what does your example make you see in the episode's argument about belonging — and what does the episode make you see in your example? Mark this as your reading.

Sources

  • The reference site (the diaspora-identity and title-echo cards): https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/
  • Omi and Winant, Racial Formation in the United States (2014), ch. 4 (from the module).

Criteria (out of 10)

Points For
4 Authority, its source, and its stakes are all named concretely
3 The scene and your example are read through each other, producing a new observation
2 The reading is labelled as yours and kept apart from documented facts
1 One page, plain prose, no padding
