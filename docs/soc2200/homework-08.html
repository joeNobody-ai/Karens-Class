Homework 8 — One intersection, one scene

Due: start of Module 9 · Length: 1 page · Weight: counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

Task

  1. Pick one moment from the Ep 7 pageant (the screened scene) and show, concretely, that the standard being applied there is neither purely racial nor purely gendered but both at once. Use Crenshaw's intersectionality: explain why "race + gender" is the wrong formula for what the moment shows.
  2. In two or three sentences, name one controlling image (Collins) that the moment summons or resists — and what work that image does for whoever is applying the standard.

Sources

  • The reference site (the Miss Gilbert Hall and cuffing-season cards): https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/
  • Crenshaw, "Demarginalizing the Intersection of Race and Sex" (1989) and Collins, Black Feminist Thought (1990), ch. 4 (from the module).

Criteria (out of 10)

Points For
4 The "fused, not added" point is demonstrated on one specific moment, not asserted in general
3 The controlling image is named and its function is explained
2 The reading is labelled as yours, distinct from the documented summary
1 One page, plain prose, no padding
