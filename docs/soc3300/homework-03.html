Homework 3 — Trace the chain (data exercise)

Due: start of Module 4 · Length: 1 page · Weight: counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

Task

This is a data exercise: you must find one real, primary figure and use it. No news articles about a study — go to the source.

  1. Find a primary figure about who makes television: e.g., the share of broadcast/cable/streaming showrunners, directors, or head writers who are women, or who are Black/people of color, in a given year. Use a primary source (the guild or industry report itself — e.g., a WGA, DGA, or UCLA Hollywood Diversity-type report).
  2. Report the figure with its source, year, and denominator (what the percentage is of — all episodes? all credited writers? all series?). If the source gives a comparison group (e.g., share of the U.S. population), give it.
  3. In one paragraph, connect the number to Module 3's question of gatekeeping: what does this figure tell you about who is positioned to make the culture the rest of us consume — and what does the figure not tell you (name at least one honest limit)?
  4. In one sentence, connect it to the case: A Different World began as a Cosby Show spin-off and was later chaired by Debbie Allen (both documented). What does your figure add to that documented fact?

Sources

  • A primary industry/guild report (you supply the citation — source, year, denominator)
  • The Cosby-Origin card: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/ep01.html#ep01-cosby-origin
  • The Debbie-Allen-Chair card: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/ep01.html#ep01-debbie-allen-chair
  • The lingo reference: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/lingo.html
  • The reference site: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/

Criteria (out of 10)

Points For
3 Source quality: a primary report, correctly cited, with year and denominator (or comparison group) given
3 The figure is used in an argument about gatekeeping, not just quoted
2 An honest limit is named — about measurement or interpretation, not a hedge
2 The figure is connected to the documented production facts of the case
