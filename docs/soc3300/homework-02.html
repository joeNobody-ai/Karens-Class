Homework 2 — Anatomy of a ritual

Due: start of Module 3 · Length: 1 page · Weight: counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

Task

  1. Choose a ritual: the homecoming step (as documented in the reference card), or one ritual from your own life (a team chant, a graduation walk, a weekly gathering). Name it and where it lives.
  2. Take it apart with Durkheim's three-part mechanism: (a) assembly — who gathers, around what shared symbol; (b) effervescence — what the doing together produces, and what evidence you would look for; (c) solidarity — the felt bond that outlasts the moment. One short paragraph each.
  3. Finish with a single sentence stating what this ritual makes that could not be made by any one person acting alone. That sentence is your thesis.

Sources

  • Émile Durkheim, The Elementary Forms of the Religious Life (1912)
  • The Homecoming-Step card: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/ep03.html#ep03-homecoming-step
  • The reference site: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/

Criteria (out of 10)

Points For
4 All three parts of the mechanism (assembly, effervescence, solidarity) are used distinctly and in order
3 The final sentence is a real thesis — a claim about what the ritual produces — not a summary
2 The ritual is concrete enough that the mechanism can be checked against it
1 Evidence discipline: documented facts (from the card) are kept apart from your own observation
