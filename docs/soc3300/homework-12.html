Homework 12 — A taste map

Due: start of Module 13 · Length: 1 page · Weight: counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

Task

  1. Pick one preference — food, music, décor, a "classy" or "basic" judgment you have actually witnessed or made. State it concretely.
  2. Draw its taste map using Bourdieu, in three short paragraphs: - Position: who shares this preference, who scorns it, and what position in social space does it signal? - Capital: which form(s) of cultural capital — institutionalized, objectified, embodied — does the preference trade on or display? - Distinction: what boundary does the preference draw, and who is kept on the outside of it?
  3. Close by answering honestly: was this preference yours (freely chosen) or your position's (produced)? Argue it — do not confess and do not dodge.

Sources

  • Pierre Bourdieu, Distinction: A Social Critique of the Judgement of Taste (1979)
  • The Dwayne–Whitley-Marriage card: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/ep03.html#ep03-dwayne-whitley-marriage
  • The reference site: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/

Criteria (out of 10)

Points For
4 Position, capital, and distinction are each applied distinctly — the preference is located, not just judged
3 The closing argument takes a real position on freedom vs. position, argued not confessed
2 The preference is concrete enough to map
1 The concept of distinction is used in both senses (refinement and separation)
