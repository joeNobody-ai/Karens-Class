Sources — SOC 3300 — Culture, Media, and Representation

Two kinds of citation are used in this course, and they are kept apart. Books and articles are cited by author, title, and year, with no links. The shows' record — episode titles, air dates, credits, and published summaries — is cited through the project's reference site, which carries its own evidence tiers and sources. Do not substitute one for the other.

1. Core readings (assigned across the term)

  • Stuart Hall, Encoding/Decoding (1973)
  • Pierre Bourdieu, Distinction: A Social Critique of the Judgement of Taste (1979)
  • Herman Gray, Watching Race: Television and the Struggle for "Blackness" (1995)

2. Concept bibliography (by module, for the instructor and ambitious students)

Theories of culture. - Clifford Geertz, The Interpretation of Cultures (1973) — thick description; culture as webs of meaning - Ann Swidler, "Culture in Action: Symbols and Strategies" (1986) — culture as a toolkit - Raymond Williams, Keywords (1976) and Culture and Society (1958) — culture as a whole way of life

Ritual and solidarity. - Émile Durkheim, The Elementary Forms of the Religious Life (1912) — collective effervescence - Randall Collins, Interaction Ritual Chains (2004) — micro-sociology of ritual

Production of culture. - Theodor W. Adorno and Max Horkheimer, Dialectic of Enlightenment (1944) — the culture industry - Howard S. Becker, Art Worlds (1982) — conventions and collective production - Richard A. Peterson and N. Anand, "The Production of Culture Perspective" (2004) - Pierre Bourdieu, The Field of Cultural Production (1993) — fields, positions, gatekeeping

Encoding, decoding, representation. - Stuart Hall, "Encoding/Decoding" (1973) and Representation: Cultural Representations and Signifying Practices (1997) - Laura Mulvey, "Visual Pleasure and Narrative Cinema" (1975) — the male gaze - bell hooks, Black Looks: Race and Representation (1992) — the oppositional gaze - John Berger, Ways of Seeing (1972) — being seen versus being watched

The sitcom and its audience. - Herman Gray, Watching Race (1995) - Robin R. Means Coleman, African American Viewers and the Black Situation Comedy: Situating Racial Humor (2000) - Sut Jhally and Justin Lewis, Enlightened Racism (1992) — the Cosby audience

Language and identity. - Geneva Smitherman, Black Talk (1994) - Rickford and Rickford, Spoken Soul: The Story of Black English (2000) - Pierre Bourdieu, Language and Symbolic Power (1991) — linguistic capital

Media effects and moral panics. - Stanley Cohen, Folk Devils and Moral Panics (1972) - George Gerbner et al., "Living with Television: The Dynamics of the Cultivation Process" (1986)

Reception and fandom. - Ien Ang, Watching Dallas (1985) - Janice Radway, Reading the Romance (1984) - Henry Jenkins, Textual Poachers: Television Fans and Participatory Culture (1992) - Michel de Certeau, The Practice of Everyday Life (1984) — "poaching"

Taste and distinction. - Pierre Bourdieu, Distinction (1979) - Herbert J. Gans, Popular Culture and High Culture (1974) — taste cultures

Memory and nostalgia. - Maurice Halbwachs, On Collective Memory (1925/1992) - Svetlana Boym, The Future of Nostalgia (2001) - Julia Kristeva, Desire in Language (1980) — intertextuality

Culture as resistance. - Dick Hebdige, Subculture: The Meaning of Style (1979) - James C. Scott, Weapons of the Weak (1985) — everyday resistance - Paul Gilroy, The Black Atlantic: Modernity and Double Consciousness (1993) — diaspora
