Homework 8 — A register and its market

Due: start of Module 9 · Length: 1 page · Weight: counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

Task

Observe the vocabulary protocol throughout: do not perform, define, or ask anyone to perform a term; discuss terms by reference, not by imitation.

  1. Pick one documented instance where register matters in the case (e.g., the diaspora-identity argument the published summary places Kojo in, or the difference between how the show's eras speak). Name the documented fact you are working from.
  2. Apply Bourdieu's linguistic market in two paragraphs: - What is the currency here — which way of speaking carries authority, and who profits? - What is the cost — which register is devalued, and what does that cost its speaker?
  3. Then name one place where code-switching would be competence (skill) and one where it would be survival (necessity), and say what decides which it is.

Sources

  • Pierre Bourdieu, Language and Symbolic Power (1991)
  • Geneva Smitherman, Black Talk (1994) — for definitions, not for performance
  • The lingo reference: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/lingo.html
  • The reference site: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/

Criteria (out of 10)

Points For
4 The linguistic-market analysis is concrete: it names a currency and a cost, not a general "language matters"
3 Competence vs. survival is genuinely distinguished, with a stated criterion for telling them apart
2 The protocol is honored — no performed or requested definitions
1 Evidence discipline: what is documented, what is period vocabulary, what is interpretation are kept apart
