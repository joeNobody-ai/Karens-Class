Homework 9 — A performed identity

Due: start of Module 10 · Length: 1 page · Weight: counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

Task

  1. Choose the homecoming step (as documented in the reference card) or the show's theme song (the "Brandy" theme, per the reference card). Name your choice.
  2. Argue, in three short paragraphs, that this is a performance that constitutes identity rather than one that merely expresses a pre-existing identity. For each paragraph, name the mechanism: - what the doing makes (Goffman's constitutive performance), - what the body carries that text cannot (embodied culture), - what music or rhythm does that speech cannot (music and identity).
  3. Close with one sentence stating the identity being constituted — the "we" the performance calls into being — and who is inside that "we" and who is outside it.

Sources

  • Erving Goffman, The Presentation of Self in Everyday Life (1959)
  • Elizabeth Fine, Soulstepping: African American Step Shows (2003)
  • The Homecoming-Step card: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/ep03.html#ep03-homecoming-step
  • The Brandy-Theme card: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/ep10.html#ep10-brandy-theme
  • The reference site: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/

Criteria (out of 10)

Points For
4 The "constitutes, not merely expresses" thesis is argued with all three mechanisms doing distinct work
3 The closing "we" is specified — insiders and outsiders named
2 The analysis stays on documented material; no imitation or invented detail
1 Precision of prose
