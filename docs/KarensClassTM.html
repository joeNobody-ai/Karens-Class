How these courses work

Every course runs on a single case — A Different World — and on the evidence rule: three kinds of claim kept apart everywhere. Documented facts (an episode title, air date, credit, or published summary), interpretations (readings, labelled as readings), and hypotheses (claims on the table for testing). The documented record of the series lives at the project's reference site: different-world-notes.
