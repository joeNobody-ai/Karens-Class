Homework 7 — A culture-versus-structure analysis

Due: start of Module 8 · Length: 1 page · Weight: counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

Task

  1. Choose one change in family life that is often called a "problem" (for example, later marriage, more single-parent households, or dual-earner couples). State, with a source, what has measurably changed.
  2. In one paragraph each, give (a) the culture account of that change — values and choices — and (b) the structure account — conditions and constraints. Then say which account explains more, and name one thing your preferred account cannot explain.

Sources

  • OpenStax, Introduction to Sociology 3e (2021), the chapter on marriage and family.
  • The reference site: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/

Criteria (out of 10)

Points For
4 The change is described as a documented fact, separated from any verdict on it
3 Both the culture and the structure accounts are given real, distinct content
2 The student takes a position and names what that position cannot explain
1 Prose is precise, with no padding
