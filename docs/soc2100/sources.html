Sources

Two kinds of citation appear in this course: books and articles (cited by author, title, and year — no links) and the shows' record (via the reference-site links, which are allowed).

Books and articles

Core readings

  • OpenStax, Introduction to Sociology 3e (Rice University, 2021)
  • Joel Best, Social Problems (any recent edition)

Concept bibliography

  • C. Wright Mills, The Sociological Imagination (1959)
  • Malcolm Spector & John I. Kitsuse, Constructing Social Problems (1977)
  • Howard S. Becker, Outsiders (1963)
  • Erving Goffman, Stigma (1963); Frame Analysis (1974)
  • William Julius Wilson, The Truly Disadvantaged (1987)
  • Michael Omi & Howard Winant, Racial Formation in the United States (1986)
  • Candace West & Don H. Zimmerman, "Doing Gender" (1987)
  • Arlie Russell Hochschild, The Second Shift (1989)
  • Patricia Hill Collins, Black Feminist Thought (1990)
  • Kimberlé Crenshaw, "Demarginalizing the Intersection of Race and Sex" (1989)
  • Peter Conrad, "The Medicalization of Society" (2007)
  • Stanley Cohen, Folk Devils and Moral Panics (1972)
  • John D. McCarthy & Mayer N. Zald, "Resource Mobilization and Social Movements" (1977)
  • David A. Snow & Robert D. Benford, "Ideology, Frame Resonance, and Participant Mobilization" (1988)
  • Randall Collins, The Credential Society (1979)
  • Philip W. Jackson, Life in Classrooms (1968)
  • Douglas S. Massey & Nancy A. Denton, American Apartheid (1993)
  • Robert J. Sampson, Great American City (2012)
