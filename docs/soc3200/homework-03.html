Homework 3 — The Wealth Gap, by the Numbers

Due: start of Module 4 · Length: 1 page · Weight: counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

Task

This is a data exercise. Find one real, current primary figure about the racial wealth gap (or about wealth inequality more broadly), and report it the way a careful reader must.

  1. Locate a primary source — a government statistical agency, a central bank, or a peer-reviewed study — and record one figure: for example, median net worth for two groups, or the share of wealth held by the top decile.
  2. Report, in one short paragraph: the figure, its source, its year, and its denominator or comparison group (e.g. "median net worth, in 2019 dollars, for households headed by…"). Then state one thing the figure cannot show, honestly — about measurement, not as a hedge.

Sources

  • Begin at a primary source of your own finding; the course does not supply the number for you.
  • The reference site for the show's money-pressure beats: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/
  • The lingo reference: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/lingo.html

Criteria (out of 10)

Points For
3 Source quality: a primary source, current, correctly described — not a news article about a study
3 Completeness: figure, source, year, and denominator or comparison group all present
2 Honesty about limits: a real measurement limit, not "it might not be accurate"
2 The figure used in one argument without overclaiming (correlation not treated as cause)
