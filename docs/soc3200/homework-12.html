Homework 12 — The Address You Were Born Into

Due: start of Module 13 · Length: 1 page · Weight: counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

Task

  1. Read Hillman as a place: name two resources attached to the address (a network, a school, a safety margin, a price of entry) that the character does not carry in herself. Anchor each to Ep 1 (or to Whitley's Last Supper).
  2. Apply neighborhood effects and opportunity hoarding, then draw the causal chain from "born at address X" to "life outcome Y," marking which link each scene lets you see and which it does not. Label your claims by tier.

Sources

  • Douglas Massey and Nancy Denton, American Apartheid (1993); Charles Tilly, Durable Inequality (1998) — your class notes.
  • The reference site's Hillman College card: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/ep01.html#ep01-hillman-college
  • https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/

Criteria (out of 10)

Points For
4 Two address-attached resources named and anchored, not "it's a nice campus"
3 Neighborhood effects and opportunity hoarding kept distinct and each put to work
2 A causal chain drawn with each link's strength honestly assessed
1 Tiers labelled; place treated as a mechanism, not scenery
