Homework 15 — Your Causal Account

Due: with the final exam · Length: 2 pages · Weight: counts in the homework average and feeds the capstone (lowest two homework dropped)

Task

This is the written capstone. Build and defend a causal account of one inequality.

  1. State your causal claim in one sentence: what produces the outcome, and for whom. Make it arguable and specific — not "inequality exists."
  2. Give the mechanism (how the cause works, in terms of at least one theory from the term) and one piece of concrete evidence anchored to a screened scene or a documented episode fact.
  3. Name the honest limit: what your account cannot show, and what one measurement would strengthen or break it. Label every claim as documented, interpretation, or hypothesis.

Sources

  • Karl Marx and Friedrich Engels, The Communist Manifesto (1848); Max Weber, Class, Status, Party (1922); Pierre Bourdieu, Distinction (1979).
  • https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/

Criteria (out of 10)

Points For
4 A plain, specific, falsifiable causal claim — unmistakably yours
3 A mechanism in theoretical terms, plus one anchored piece of evidence
2 The honest limit named (about the claim, not a hedge)
1 Prose a non-specialist could read; tiers visible throughout
