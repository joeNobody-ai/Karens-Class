Homework 4 — Taste as Class

Due: start of Module 5 · Length: 1 page · Weight: counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

Task

  1. From the headliner beat in Ep 3, Center de Stage, list two judgements of taste you can observe — a preference, a dismissal, a claim about what is "right" or "good." Describe each with an anchor to the beat.
  2. Read each judgement through distinction and habitus: what position does the judgement mark, and where does the taste come from? Label each of your claims as documented, interpretation, or hypothesis.

Criteria (out of 10)

Points For
4 Two concrete, anchored judgements of taste — not a retelling of the episode
3 Distinction and habitus each made to do distinct analytical work
2 The "taste never names money" mechanism identified in the beat
1 Tiers labelled on your own claims; prose is clean
