Homework 14 — The Ladder Across Borders

Due: start of Module 15 · Length: 1 page · Weight: counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

Task

  1. From the diaspora argument in Ep 7 (or from A World Alike), identify the position inside the identity — the ranking that the argument about culture is also an argument about. Anchor it.
  2. Apply the world system (core/periphery) and stratified migration to explain the position: who moves, who was moved, and what that movement costs. Label each claim as documented, interpretation, or hypothesis.

Sources

  • Immanuel Wallerstein, The Modern World-System (1974) — your class notes.
  • The reference site's diaspora-identity card: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/ep07.html#ep07-diaspora-identity
  • https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/

Criteria (out of 10)

Points For
4 A position found inside the identity argument, anchored — not "culture vs. economics" as a false split
3 Core/periphery used as a relational claim, not a list of rich and poor places
2 Migration treated as stratified (open to some, gated for others), not as a neutral act
1 Tiers labelled; no collapsing of diaspora to a single story
