# Homework 11 — What your institution teaches

**Due:** start of Session 12 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Apply the "hidden curriculum" to an institution you actually sit inside — your college, school, or
   workplace.
2. Answer in a compact block:
   - **The written lesson:** what the institution says it teaches (its syllabus, its mission).
   - **The hidden lesson:** one thing it teaches by *how it is arranged* — who speaks, who is deferred to,
     what is rewarded, what is punished.
   - **Who it favours:** who the hidden curriculum advantages.
   - **The gate:** one way the institution sorts people (a credential, a test, a rank) and where the sorted
     people end up.
