# Homework 10 — Personal or structural?

**Due:** start of Session 11 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Take one relationship — from the week's material (Ep 4's "cuffing season", the parents' marriage) or
   from life — and ask the course's standing question: *is this personal, structural, or both?*
2. Answer in a compact block:
   - **The relationship:** who it is between, in one sentence.
   - **What is personal:** the part that is about the two people themselves.
   - **What is structural:** the part that was shaped before they met (class, geography, education, the
     "marriage market", family form).
   - **Your judgment:** which matters more here, and what evidence you would need to be sure.
