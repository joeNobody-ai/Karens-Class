# Homework 12 — Read an image

**Due:** start of Session 13 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Pick **one image** of a community — a poster, a clip, a casting announcement, a scene from the show, or
   any media depiction.
2. Read it the way Session 12 read the show, in a compact block:
   - **Who made it:** the producer of the image (a person, a studio, a platform) — and what they are trying
     to accomplish.
   - **Who it is for:** the intended audience, and how you can tell.
   - **What it emphasises:** what the framing puts at the centre.
   - **What it omits:** what is left out of the frame, and what changes if you add it back.

## Sources
