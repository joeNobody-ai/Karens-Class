# Homework 8 — A category under pressure

**Due:** start of Session 9 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Take the week's question — *who belongs to "Black," and who decides?* — to one concrete case.
2. Choose one of:
   - the diaspora argument in Ep 7 (first half), or
   - a real-world case of the same tension (who counts as Black in a census, a movement, or an institution).
3. Answer in a compact block:
   - **The category:** what "Black" (or the relevant category) is being asked to mean.
   - **Who defines it:** who has the power to draw the line.
   - **Who is written out:** the party whose interests a tight definition leaves out.
   - **What it costs them:** the concrete consequence.
