# Homework 4 — An agent of your own socialization

**Due:** start of Session 5 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Name one **agent of socialization** in your own life (a person, an institution, a peer group, a piece of
   media) that taught you a norm or a role you now follow.
2. Answer in a compact block:
   - **The agent:** who or what it was.
   - **What it taught:** the specific norm, value, or role.
   - **How:** the mechanism — instruction, example, reward, correction, imitation.
   - **Was it anticipatory?** Were you learning for a role you did not yet hold? Say why or why not.
3. One sentence on how Session 4's "aunties" example compares to your own case.
