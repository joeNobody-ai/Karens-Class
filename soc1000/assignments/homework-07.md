# Homework 7 — Find the number

**Due:** start of Session 8 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

This is a data exercise: go get one real number and report it honestly.

1. Pick one claim about inequality that Session 7 raised — for example: the wealth gap, student debt,
   college costs, or who graduates with what.
2. Find **one primary figure** (from a government agency, a research institute, or an academic source —
   not a news article *about* a study).
3. Report it in a compact block:
   - **The figure:** the number, exactly as the source states it.
   - **The source:** who published it, and the year.
   - **The denominator or comparison:** what it is a fraction or comparison *of*.
   - **One honest limit:** one thing this number cannot tell you.
