# Homework 9 — A script and its break

**Due:** start of Session 10 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Identify one **gender script** — the expected way a man or a woman should behave in a scene (a date, a
   pageant, a breakup, a workplace, a classroom).
2. Use a scene from the week's material, or one from your own observation.
3. Answer in a compact block:
   - **The script:** what the expected performance is.
   - **Who enforces it:** who holds the performer to the standard, and how.
   - **The break:** a moment where someone deviates from the script — what happens when they do.
   - **Intersection:** one way race or class changes how this script plays out.
