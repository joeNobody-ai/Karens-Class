# Homework 3 — A norm in the wild

**Due:** start of Session 4 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Pick one **norm** you saw enforced in Session 3's material (the homecoming / step routine) — or one from
   your own campus or community.
2. Answer in a compact block:
   - **The norm:** what the rule actually is, stated plainly.
   - **The value behind it:** what the group says is good, that this norm protects.
   - **The boundary:** who is inside and who is outside because of it.
   - **Who polices it:** who enforces the rule, and what happens to someone who breaks it.
