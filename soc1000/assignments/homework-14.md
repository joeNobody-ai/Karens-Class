# Homework 14 — One scene, three concepts

**Due:** start of Session 15 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Pick **one scene** from the finale (Ep 10, *A Gift & The Curse*) — or, if you prefer, one scene from
   anywhere in the season you have not yet written about.
2. Read it with **three different concepts** from the term (sociological imagination, culture, socialization,
   groups, deviance, stratification, race, gender, family, education, media, movements).
3. Write a compact reading:
   - **The scene:** one sentence describing it (your observation, not a quote).
   - **Concept 1 / 2 / 3:** for each, what the concept makes visible in the scene that the other two do not.
   - **The combination:** one sentence on what the three *together* show that any one alone cannot.

## Sources

- The whole term's concepts (Sessions 1–13)
- The reference site: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/
