# Homework 6 — A label and its cost

**Due:** start of Session 7 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Choose one label that gets applied to a person or a group (a stigma, a "deviant" label, a master status)
   — from the week's material or from the world around you.
2. Answer in a compact block:
   - **The label:** what it is, stated plainly.
   - **Who applies it:** who has the power to make it stick.
   - **The cost:** what the labelled person loses or carries because of it (standing, access, safety, a
     future).
   - **Medical or moral?** Is the label treated as a medical condition or a moral failing — and what
     difference does that make?
