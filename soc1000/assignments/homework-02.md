# Homework 2 — Sorting the evidence

**Due:** start of Session 3 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Open the reference site's callback index: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/#callbacks
2. Choose **three** callbacks from **three different episodes**.
3. For each, answer in a compact block:
   - **What is claimed?** (one sentence, your own words)
   - **Tier:** documented, interpretation, or hypothesis. Say why.
   - **The card's own label:** what tier does the reference site give it, and do you agree?
   - **What would change your mind?** One concrete piece of evidence you could actually obtain.
4. Finish with two sentences: which of your three is the strongest claim, and what distinguishes it from the weakest.

## Sources

- The callbacks themselves, each with its own sources: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/
- Sessions 1 and 2.

## Criteria (out of 10)

| Points | For |

| 3 | "What would change your mind" is concrete and obtainable |
| 2 | Three different episodes; claims in your own words |
| 1 | The closing comparison makes a real distinction |

**Do not** quote dialogue. The published summaries are the evidence base; if you have watched an episode and want to cite a moment, describe it and mark it as *your observation*.
