# Homework 5 — Map a group you belong to

**Due:** start of Session 6 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Pick one group you actually belong to (a team, a club, a workplace, a friend group, a family, a faith
   community).
2. Classify it, in a compact block:
   - **Primary or secondary?** (face-to-face and emotionally close, or goal-oriented and impersonal — or
     somewhere between.) Say why.
   - **In-group or out-group?** Name the group you define it *against*, if there is one.
   - **Formal or informal?** Does it have written rules and a hierarchy, or unwritten ones?
   - **Reference group?** Is there a group you measure yourself against without belonging to it? Name it.
3. One sentence on what the group *does to* the people in it — using Session 5's terms.
