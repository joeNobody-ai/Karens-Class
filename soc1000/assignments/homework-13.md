# Homework 13 — Frame a grievance

**Due:** start of Session 14 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Take one real grievance — from the week's material (the sit-in, the call-out, the divestment fight) or
   from a cause you care about.
2. Write its **three frames**, in Session 13's terms:
   - **Diagnostic frame:** what is wrong, and who or what is responsible.
   - **Prognostic frame:** what should be done about it.
   - **Motivational frame:** why people should act — what makes it worth it.
3. Then answer: **what resource does this movement need that it probably does not have**, and **who bears
   the cost** of acting?
