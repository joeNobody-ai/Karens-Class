# Session 15 — The Sociological Perspective (Capstone and Review)

**Big question.** If the case is American, what follows for the discipline?

**Where we start.** The course ends where it began — with the show — but now with the whole toolkit in
hand. The premise underneath it all: Black experience is not a topic bolted onto "general" sociology; it
is one of the places where general theory is tested. This session is the capstone, the review, and the
handing-over of the method.

## Sociological frame

1. **Review**: the fifteen modules, restated as one method — see a scene, name the structure, separate
   the documented from the interpreted, connect it to the general.
2. **The premise**: Black experience is where general theory gets tested. The case is not a "special
   topic"; it is the terrain on which the discipline's general claims are tried and revised.
3. **Public-facing writing**: the capstone's skill — say something precise and true to a reader who has
   not taken this course.
4. **The sociological perspective, kept**: a habit you carry out of the room, not a fact you memorised.

## The 75 minutes

| Min | What |

| 45–60 | **The premise, argued.** Is the case a "special topic" or the test of the general? Take the strongest objection seriously — that a sitcom cannot bear the weight — and answer it |

## Discussion questions

1. Which module changed how you see something you previously took for granted?
2. What is the strongest objection to this course — and what is the strongest answer?
3. Is *A Different World* a special topic or a general case? Argue from the term's evidence.
4. What is one concept you will still use in five years?
5. What should a first-year leave this course able to *do*?

## Instructor notes

**If the room is quiet during presentations.** The bar is low and stated: claim, evidence, one unsettled
question. Nobody is being asked to perform; the prompt is on the board.

**If the "is it a special topic" debate stalls.** Reframe as a question about the discipline: what would
be lost from sociology if it had never studied Black life? The answer to *that* is the answer to the module.

**The self-assessment.** Hand out the five questions (in `rubrics.md`) and collect before the final. It is
the best course evaluation you will get, and it takes five minutes.
