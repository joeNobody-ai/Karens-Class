# Sources — SOC 3200 Social Stratification and Inequality

Citations in this course come in two kinds, kept separate on purpose: **books and articles** (author,
title, year — no links) and **the shows' record** (via the reference-site links, which are allowed
because the reference site is the course's evidence base).

## Core readings

These three carry the theoretical spine of the course and are returned to across modules:

- Karl Marx and Friedrich Engels, *The Communist Manifesto* (1848).
- Max Weber, *Class, Status, Party* (1922).
- Pierre Bourdieu, *Distinction: A Social Critique of the Judgement of Taste* (1979).

## Concept bibliography (by module)

- **Stratification, life chances, persistence, structure** — Weber, *Class, Status, Party* (1922); Göran
  Therborn, *The Killing Fields of Inequality* (2013).
- **Marx and class; Weber and status; class, status, party; exploitation** — Marx and Engels, *The
  Communist Manifesto* (1848); Marx, *Capital, Vol. I* (1867); Weber, *Class, Status, Party* (1922).
- **Wealth, income, the racial wealth gap, assets, debt** — Thomas Piketty, *Capital in the Twenty-First
  Century* (2014); Melvin Oliver and Thomas Shapiro, *Black Wealth / White Wealth* (1995).
- **Bourdieu; cultural capital; distinction; habitus** — Bourdieu, *Distinction* (1979); Bourdieu, "The
  Forms of Capital" (1986).
- **The Black middle class; intraracial class; Frazier** — E. Franklin Frazier, *Black Bourgeoisie*
  (1957); Mary Pattillo, *Black Picket Fences* (1999); Karyn Lacy, *Blue-Chip Black* (2007).
- **Reproduction; credentialing; Bourdieu and Passeron** — Bourdieu and Jean-Claude Passeron,
  *Reproduction in Education, Society and Culture* (1977); Randall Collins, *The Credential Society*
  (1979).
- **Racial stratification; racial formation; segmented labor markets** — Michael Omi and Howard Winant,
  *Racial Formation in the United States* (1986); William Julius Wilson, *The Declining Significance of
  Race* (1978).
- **Gender stratification; the second shift; occupational segregation** — Arlie Hochschild, *The Second
  Shift* (1989); Barbara Reskin and Patricia Roos, *Job Queues, Gender Queues* (1990).
- **Intergenerational mobility; inheritance; family and class** — Annette Lareau, *Unequal Childhoods*
  (2003); Raj Chetty et al., "The Fading American Dream" (2017).
- **Labor markets; precarity; the gig economy; deskilling** — Harry Braverman, *Labor and Monopoly
  Capital* (1974); Guy Standing, *The Precariat* (2011).
- **Health gradient; fundamental cause theory; stress and status** — Michael Marmot, *The Status
  Syndrome* (2004); Bruce Link and Jo Phelan, "Social Conditions as Fundamental Causes of Disease"
  (1995).
- **Residential segregation; neighborhood effects; place; opportunity hoarding** — Douglas Massey and
  Nancy Denton, *American Apartheid* (1993); William Julius Wilson, *The Truly Disadvantaged* (1987);
  Charles Tilly, *Durable Inequality* (1998).
- **Elites; power; institutional reproduction** — C. Wright Mills, *The Power Elite* (1956); G. William
  Domhoff, *Who Rules America?* (1967).
- **Global stratification; the world system; diaspora; migration** — Immanuel Wallerstein, *The Modern
  World-System* (1974); Saskia Sassen, *The Mobility of Labor and Capital* (1988).
