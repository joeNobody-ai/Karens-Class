# Homework 15 — Your Causal Account

**Due:** with the final exam · **Length:** 2 pages · **Weight:** counts in the homework average and feeds the capstone (lowest two homework dropped)

## Task

This is the written capstone. Build and defend a **causal account** of one inequality.

1. State your **causal claim** in one sentence: what produces the outcome, and for whom. Make it arguable
   and specific — not "inequality exists."
2. Give the **mechanism** (how the cause works, in terms of at least one theory from the term) and **one
   piece of concrete evidence** anchored to a screened scene or a documented episode fact.
3. Name the **honest limit**: what your account cannot show, and what one measurement would strengthen
   or break it. Label every claim as documented, interpretation, or hypothesis.
