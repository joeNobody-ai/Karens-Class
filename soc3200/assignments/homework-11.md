# Homework 11 — The Gradient in One Scene

**Due:** start of Module 12 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. From Ep 5, *Final Words* (or from *If I Should Die Before I Wake*), identify **one point on the health
   gradient** — a place where a character's position meets a health exposure. Anchor it.
2. Apply **fundamental cause theory** to explain *why* that point sits where it does, and then state,
   precisely, why a single scene can show only a **point**, never the **gradient** itself. Label your
   claims by tier.
