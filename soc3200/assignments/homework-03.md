# Homework 3 — The Wealth Gap, by the Numbers

**Due:** start of Module 4 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

This is a data exercise. Find **one real, current primary figure** about the racial wealth gap (or about
wealth inequality more broadly), and report it the way a careful reader must.

1. Locate a primary source — a government statistical agency, a central bank, or a peer-reviewed study —
   and record **one figure**: for example, median net worth for two groups, or the share of wealth held by
   the top decile.
2. Report, in one short paragraph: the **figure**, its **source**, its **year**, and its **denominator or
   comparison group** (e.g. "median net worth, in 2019 dollars, for households headed by…"). Then state
   one thing the figure *cannot* show, honestly — about measurement, not as a hedge.
