# Homework 2 — Marx vs. Weber at Hillman

**Due:** start of Module 3 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Take one beat from Ep 9, *Romancing the Unknown*, and write one paragraph reading it through **Marx**:
   who owns, who must sell their labour, and where the surplus goes.
2. Write a second paragraph reading the *same* beat through **Weber**: map the ranking onto class, status,
   and party, and name which axis is doing the most work.
3. Close with one sentence each: what Marx's lens cannot see in the beat, and what Weber's cannot.

## Sources

- Karl Marx and Friedrich Engels, *The Communist Manifesto* (1848); Max Weber, *Class, Status, Party* (1922) — your class notes.
- https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/
