# SOC 3300 — Final Examination — Answer Key (INSTRUCTOR MATERIAL)

**Do not distribute to students.** This key states what a full-credit answer must contain and the
marker's notes. It does not script a single "correct" answer; credit is for the *structure* of the
analysis, not for matching wording.

## Part A — 12 points each (60 total)

### A1 — Two finales, two nostalgias (12)

| Points | Full-credit answer must contain |
|---|---|
| 4 | Boym's restorative/reflective distinction stated accurately and Halbwachs's "memory is reconstructed for present purposes" stated |
| 4 | Each finale assigned a *kind* of closing, argued (not merely asserted) with reference to the documented facts — the gala/breaking points/decisions, and the 1993-07-02 air date |
| 4 | The season-vs.-series distinction made: what a *series* finale owes (closing a decade, a "whole") that a season finale does not |

**Common errors:** defining Boym but never actually choosing a kind for each finale; treating "finale" as
self-evidently nostalgic; no use of Halbwachs's *present-purpose* point.

### A2 — Three capitals, one display (12)

| Points | Full-credit answer must contain |
|---|---|
| 4 | All three forms of capital named correctly and distinguished (institutionalized = credentials; objectified = objects; embodied = accent/ease/know-how) |
| 4 | At least one documented fact applied, with the capital form(s) it trades on identified |
| 4 | A boundary named — who is inside and who is kept outside — tied to the display |

**Common errors:** conflating the three capitals; treating capital as "money" only; naming a boundary
with no account of *who* is excluded.

### A3 — Hall meets Cohen (12)

| Points | Full-credit answer must contain |
|---|---|
| 4 | Hall's core claim stated (meaning is produced at encoding and decoding, which need not coincide) and Cohen's (alarm is patterned, disproportionate, and itself consequential) |
| 4 | Both used on the *same* documented fact (the called-out speech), not serially on different facts |
| 4 | A stated seam: where decoding ends and panic begins — an account of how a contested reading becomes an alarm |

**Common errors:** presenting the two as the same idea; answering about Hall then about Cohen in separate
paragraphs with no integration; no account of the *transition* from reading to panic.

### A4 — Culture constitutes identity (12)

| Points | Full-credit answer must contain |
|---|---|
| 4 | Two of the three (performance / embodied culture / oppositional gaze) stated accurately |
| 4 | Both applied to the *same* named documented fact (episode and air date given) |
| 4 | The contrast shown: what each reveals that the other cannot |

**Common errors:** treating "performance" and "the gaze" as interchangeable; no shared fact, so no real
comparison; asserting that identity is "constructed" without a mechanism.

### A5 — Who owns the image across time (12)

| Points | Full-credit answer must contain |
|---|---|
| 4 | Three one-sentence statements: Gray's double burden, Gilroy's diaspora-as-route, and the aunties' arrival as audience-becoming-text |
| 4 | A *defended* choice of which best explains the original cast's arrival |
| 4 | The other two are acknowledged and given their due — the answer says why the chosen one wins, not that the others are wrong |

**Common errors:** a three-way tie (no choice defended); no reference to the documented fact of the
arrival; attributing positions to the wrong authors.

## Part B — Scene analysis (40)

| Points | Full-credit answer must contain |
|---|---|
| 8 | The documented fact stated accurately and *separately* (title, air date, published summary), visibly divided from the interpretation |
| 10 | Three concepts from three *different* modules, each doing distinct work on the same scene |
| 8 | Structure and agency both identified, and the course's central tension named |
| 6 | One honest limit — what the scene cannot show — named precisely, not hedged |
| 5 | Evidence tiers visible throughout |
| 3 | Readings cited for the work they do, not decoratively |

**Common errors:** a scene *summary* instead of an analysis; three concepts that are three names for the
same observation; "limit" as a hedge ("we can't know for sure"); invented plot details presented as
documented fact; structure named but agency dropped (or vice versa).

Grade the evidence discipline first (rubric 4 in `rubrics.md`), then the concepts, then the prose. A
cumulative final earns more for *connection across modules* than for isolated recall — reward the
