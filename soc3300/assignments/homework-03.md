# Homework 3 — Trace the chain (data exercise)

**Due:** start of Module 4 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

This is a **data exercise**: you must find one real, primary figure and use it. No news articles *about*
a study — go to the source.

1. Find a primary figure about **who makes television**: e.g., the share of broadcast/cable/streaming
   showrunners, directors, or head writers who are women, or who are Black/people of color, in a given
   year. Use a primary source (the guild or industry report itself — e.g., a WGA, DGA, or UCLA Hollywood
   Diversity-type report).
2. Report the figure with its **source, year, and denominator** (what the percentage is *of* — all
   episodes? all credited writers? all series?). If the source gives a comparison group (e.g., share of
   the U.S. population), give it.
3. In one paragraph, connect the number to Module 3's question of **gatekeeping**: what does this figure
   tell you about who is positioned to make the culture the rest of us consume — and what does the figure
   *not* tell you (name at least one honest limit)?
4. In one sentence, connect it to the case: *A Different World* began as a *Cosby Show* spin-off and was
   later chaired by Debbie Allen (both documented). What does your figure add to that documented fact?
