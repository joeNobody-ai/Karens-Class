# Homework 11 — A community reads together

**Due:** start of Module 12 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Take the documented fact that in *Insomnia and the Daydreamer*, "the aunties" (the original cast)
   arrive at a club night and it "goes chaotic" (per the published summary). State that fact, then stop —
   no invented detail.
2. Read that arrival as a meeting of **two interpretive communities** (Fish): the original audience
   (holders of four decades of memory) and the new students (holders of the present). In two paragraphs,
   argue what each community would read *differently* in the same scene, and why.
3. Then apply **fandom** (Jenkins): is the arrival an act of *poaching* — the audience appropriating the
   text — or of *authority* — the originals reclaiming it? Take a side and defend it.
4. Close with one sentence on what the scene suggests about who *owns* a text once an audience has lived
   with it.
