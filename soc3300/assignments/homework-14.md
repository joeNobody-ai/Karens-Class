# Homework 14 — A cultural refusal

**Due:** start of Module 15 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Choose one cultural act that doubles as resistance: the diaspora-identity argument (per the published
   summary of *Sometimes You Get the Culture...Sometimes the Culture Gets You*), the student organizing
   the reference card documents for *Romancing the Unknown*, or one from your own life. Name the
   documented fact you start from.
2. Argue **when a cultural act is a political act**, in three short paragraphs:
   - **Hebdige:** where is the *style* a coded refusal — what is being refused, and in what idiom?
   - **Scott:** where is the resistance *everyday* and low-visibility, rather than open revolt?
   - **Gilroy / Hall:** how does a *route* (rather than *root*) model of identity itself refuse fixed,
     exclusive definitions of the group?
3. Close by naming the risk: what happens to a cultural refusal when the dominant culture *absorbs* it —
   and is absorption defeat or a strange kind of victory?
