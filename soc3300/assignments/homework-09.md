# Homework 9 — A performed identity

**Due:** start of Module 10 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Choose the homecoming step (as documented in the reference card) or the show's theme song (the
   "Brandy" theme, per the reference card). Name your choice.
2. Argue, in three short paragraphs, that this is a **performance that constitutes identity** rather than
   one that merely *expresses* a pre-existing identity. For each paragraph, name the mechanism:
   - what the doing *makes* (Goffman's constitutive performance),
   - what the body carries that text cannot (embodied culture),
   - what music or rhythm does that speech cannot (music and identity).
3. Close with one sentence stating the identity being constituted — the "we" the performance calls into
   being — and who is inside that "we" and who is outside it.
