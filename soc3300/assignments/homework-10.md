# Homework 10 — A panic in miniature

**Due:** start of Module 11 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Choose one object of alarm from the course: Deborah's public speech being "called out" (per the
   published summary of *O Captain! My Captain!*), or the media framing of Los Angeles the reference
   card documents under the "Rodney King precedent." Name your choice and the documented fact you start
   from.
2. Test it against Cohen's anatomy of a **moral panic** in three short paragraphs:
   - Is there a **folk devil** — a person or group cast as a threat? Who?
   - Is the alarm **disproportionate** to the documented facts? By what measure (name it, don't assume
     it)?
   - What does the panic **do** — who benefits from the alarm, and what gets out of frame?
3. Close by answering the module's question directly: in your case, is the media changing *behavior*, or
   changing *fear* — and what is the difference that makes it matter?
