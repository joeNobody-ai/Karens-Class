# Homework 2 — Anatomy of a ritual

**Due:** start of Module 3 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Choose a ritual: the homecoming step (as documented in the reference card), or one ritual from your
   own life (a team chant, a graduation walk, a weekly gathering). Name it and where it lives.
2. Take it apart with Durkheim's three-part mechanism: (a) **assembly** — who gathers, around what
   shared symbol; (b) **effervescence** — what the doing together produces, and what evidence you would
   look for; (c) **solidarity** — the felt bond that outlasts the moment. One short paragraph each.
3. Finish with a single sentence stating what this ritual *makes* that could not be made by any one
   person acting alone. That sentence is your thesis.

## Sources

- Émile Durkheim, *The Elementary Forms of the Religious Life* (1912)
