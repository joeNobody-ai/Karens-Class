# Homework 4 — Three readings of one scene

**Due:** start of Module 5 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Choose one documented scene from the course so far (e.g., Deborah's public speech in *O Captain! My
   Captain!*, or the show-within-the-show of *The Show Must Go On*). Name the episode and the documented
   fact you are working from.
2. Write **three readings** of that scene using Hall's decoding positions:
   - **Preferred (dominant-hegemonic):** the reading the scene is organized to invite.
   - **Negotiated:** accept the frame in part, resist it in part — say which part is which.
   - **Oppositional:** reject the frame outright and re-code the scene against its grain.
   One short paragraph each, clearly labeled.
3. In one closing sentence, state which of the three readings *you* find most defensible and why — this
   is an argument, not a preference poll.
