# Homework 12 — A taste map

**Due:** start of Module 13 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Pick one *preference* — food, music, décor, a "classy" or "basic" judgment you have actually
   witnessed or made. State it concretely.
2. Draw its **taste map** using Bourdieu, in three short paragraphs:
   - **Position:** who shares this preference, who scorns it, and what position in social space does it
     signal?
   - **Capital:** which form(s) of cultural capital — institutionalized, objectified, embodied — does the
     preference trade on or display?
   - **Distinction:** what boundary does the preference draw, and who is kept on the outside of it?
3. Close by answering honestly: was this preference *yours* (freely chosen) or *your position's*
   (produced)? Argue it — do not confess and do not dodge.
