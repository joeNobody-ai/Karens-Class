# Homework 7 — Whose image, whose burden

**Due:** start of Module 8 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Restate Herman Gray's argument in your own words: the Black sitcom is not a *window* onto Black life
   but an *argument* about blackness, made under a specific set of conditions. Keep it to three
   sentences.
2. Apply it to the case: *A Different World* began as a *Cosby Show* spin-off and was later chaired by
   Debbie Allen (both documented). Using those two documented facts, argue how the *conditions of
   production* shaped what the genre was asked to carry — and to whom.
3. Then bring in Robin Means Coleman: the audience is not unanimous. In one paragraph, describe two
   *different* legitimate things two different viewers might want from the same episode, and what that
   does to any single claim about the show's "meaning."
4. Close with one sentence naming the **double burden** this genre carried that its white counterparts
   rarely did.
