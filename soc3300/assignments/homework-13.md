# Homework 13 — What the callback remembers

**Due:** start of Module 14 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Take one documented **callback**: the revival's *Irreconcilable Differences* echoing the original's
   S1E1 *Reconcilable Differences* (1987-09-24), or the return of "the aunties" (per the published
   summary of *Insomnia and the Daydreamer*). State the documented fact.
2. Analyze what the callback **remembers**, in three short paragraphs:
   - **Halbwachs:** whose memory is being reconstructed here, by whom, and for what present purpose?
   - **Boym:** is this *restorative* nostalgia (rebuild the past) or *reflective* nostalgia (dwell on the
     distance)? Argue which, with evidence.
   - **Kristeva:** what prior text is this callback a "tissue" of, and what meaning is produced only *in
     the relation* between the two?
3. Close with one sentence on what the callback *costs* the viewer who does not hold the memory — and
   whether that exclusion is a bug or the point.
