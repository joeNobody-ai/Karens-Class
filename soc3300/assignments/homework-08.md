**Due:** start of Module 9 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

Observe the **vocabulary protocol** throughout: do not perform, define, or ask anyone to perform a term;
discuss terms by reference, not by imitation.

1. Pick one documented instance where *register* matters in the case (e.g., the diaspora-identity
   argument the published summary places Kojo in, or the difference between how the show's eras speak).
   Name the documented fact you are working from.
2. Apply Bourdieu's **linguistic market** in two paragraphs:

3. Then name one place where **code-switching** would be *competence* (skill) and one where it would be
   *survival* (necessity), and say what decides which it is.
