# Homework 5 — A scene of looking

**Due:** start of Module 6 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Choose a scene structured by *looking*: Hazel's Miss Gilbert Hall campaign (per the published summary
   of *Sometimes You Get the Culture...Sometimes the Culture Gets You*), the pageant of *Mr. Hillman*, or
   a comparable scene of display from your own viewing. Name the documented fact you are working from.
2. Map the scene with three questions, one paragraph each:
   - **Who looks?** Who is positioned as the viewer/judge?
   - **Who is looked at?** Who is on display, and on whose terms?
   - **Who controls the frame?** Who sets the rules of being seen?
3. Then apply Berger's *seen versus watched* distinction and hooks's *oppositional gaze*: is the person
   on display a *subject* (choosing to be seen) or an *object* (being watched), and is there room for
   looking back? Take a position and defend it.
