# Homework 6 — Form does work

**Due:** start of Module 7 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Take one half-hour sitcom episode from the course (the revival's *Irreconcilable Differences* or the
   original *Gift of the Magi*, 1987-12-17). Name it.
2. Map its **structure** as the sitcom requires it: the recurring *situation*, the *complication* that
   disturbs it, the *crisis*, and the *restoration* of equilibrium by the end. One short paragraph
   naming each beat, working only from the published summary and the reference card — no invented plot.
3. Argue what the form *does*: what does this structure make the episode able to say (about problems,
   about how they resolve), and what does it make hard or impossible to say? Name at least one thing the
   half-hour form forecloses.
