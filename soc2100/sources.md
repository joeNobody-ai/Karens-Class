## The shows' record

Both eras are documented on the reference project's site. The episode cards carry each episode's title, air date, and published summary, and — where the summary supports it — notes on what a scene documents versus what a viewer reads into it.

- Reference site (Netflix-style cards): https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/
- Reference site (text view): https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/
- Vocabulary reference: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/lingo.html

## Attribution and use

*A Different World* (1987–1993 and 2026) is the property of its rights holders. This course reproduces no scripts, stills, or footage, and quotes no dialogue. Episode titles, air dates, and summaries are cited from the reference project's documented pages, which carry their own sources. Statistics cited in the course belong to their sources and are flagged as "check before you teach" items; students are required to verify any figure they use against a primary source.
