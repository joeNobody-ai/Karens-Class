# Homework 10 — A framing analysis of one story

**Due:** start of Module 11 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Choose one news story about a social problem and identify its frame: what kind of thing is the story telling us this problem *is* (a crisis, an individual failing, a structural failure, a threat)? Quote the language that does the framing.
2. Re-frame the same facts: rewrite the story's headline or lede in one sentence so that it frames the problem as structural rather than individual (or vice versa). Then say, in one sentence, what changes when the frame changes.

## Sources

- Stanley Cohen, *Folk Devils and Moral Panics* (1972), or Erving Goffman, *Frame Analysis* (1974).
- The reference site: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/

| Points | For |
|---|---|

| 3 | The re-frame genuinely changes the frame, not just the wording |
