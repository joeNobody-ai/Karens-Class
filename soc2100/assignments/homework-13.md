# Homework 13 — A framing-and-mobilization analysis

**Due:** start of Module 14 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Choose one current social movement or campaign. Identify its frame — how it defines the grievance, whom it blames, and what remedy it proposes — quoting its own language.
2. Apply resource mobilization: name at least two resources the movement has (numbers, money, media, legitimacy, organization) and one it lacks. End with one sentence proposing a response to the movement's grievance, and name who bears its cost.

## Sources

- John D. McCarthy & Mayer N. Zald, "Resource Mobilization and Social Movements" (1977).
- The reference site: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/
