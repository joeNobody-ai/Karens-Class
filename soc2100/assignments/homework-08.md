# Homework 8 — A labeling trace

**Due:** start of Module 9 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Choose one behavior that is labelled a crime or a deviance in some contexts and not in others (for example, a protest action, a drug, a way of dressing, or a form of speech). State, with a source, how it is currently labelled and by whom.
2. Trace the label's career using Becker: the act, the audience's response, and what the label then *does* to the person's opportunities — independent of the act itself. Mark documented fact and interpretation apart.

## Sources

- Howard S. Becker, *Outsiders* (1963).
- The reference site: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/
