# Homework 5 — A stigma or medicalization case note

**Due:** start of Module 6 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Choose one condition that has shifted between being treated as a moral failing, a crime, or an ordinary fact and being treated as an illness (for example, addiction, certain mental illnesses, or a physical condition with a moral history). State, with a source, how it is currently labelled.
2. In one paragraph, use Goffman or Conrad to explain what the *label* does: who is stigmatized or relieved by the current framing, and who gains or loses from a change in how the condition is named. Mark documented fact and interpretation apart.

## Sources

- Erving Goffman, *Stigma* (1963), or Peter Conrad, "The Medicalization of Society" (2007).
