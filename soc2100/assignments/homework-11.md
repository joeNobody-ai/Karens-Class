# Homework 11 — A minimum-wage-versus-living-wage data exercise

**Due:** start of Module 12 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Find two primary figures for one place: (a) the current minimum wage, and (b) an estimate of the living wage or the cost of basic needs for a single adult (rent, food, transport, utilities). Record both figures, their source, their year, and the unit (hourly, monthly, or annual).
2. In one paragraph, use the gap between the two figures to argue why a person can work full-time and still be poor. Name one thing the comparison cannot show (for example, that a living wage estimate depends on assumptions about household size).
