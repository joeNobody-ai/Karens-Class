# Homework 1 — A claims-making observation log

**Due:** start of Module 2 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Choose one condition currently being discussed as a "social problem" (in the news, on campus, or in your community). For that one condition, identify: (a) who is making the claim, (b) what they say the problem is, and (c) what they demand should be done. Quote or cite the source for each part.
2. In one paragraph, apply the constructionist question: is the condition itself the problem, or is the problem the claim being made about it? Mark clearly which of your sentences are documented and which are your interpretation.

## Sources

- Joel Best, *Social Problems* (any recent edition), ch. 1–2 on claims-making.
- The reference site: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/

| Points | For |
|---|---|

| 3 | The constructionist question is actually applied, not just restated |
| 2 | Documented fact and interpretation are clearly separated |
| 1 | Prose is precise, with no padding |
