# Homework 15 — The capstone: naming a problem, defending a response

**Due:** at the final · **Length:** 2 pages · **Weight:** capstone grade (10% of the course)

## Task

1. Name one social problem precisely. State: (a) what the condition is, (b) who is claiming it is a problem and what they demand, and (c) what your account leaves out. Ground the condition in a documented episode fact or a primary source, and mark interpretation as yours.
2. Propose and defend one response to that problem. State: who it would benefit, what it would cost, and who bears that cost. Use at least two concepts from the course, each doing distinct work, and cite one reading or source for something (not decoratively).

## Sources

- The reference site: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/
- Your chosen sources, cited in full.
