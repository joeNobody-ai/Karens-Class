# Homework 9 — A boundary-of-belonging analysis

**Due:** start of Module 10 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Choose one live argument about who "belongs" somewhere — who counts as a member of a community, a diaspora, a campus, or a nation. State, with a source, what boundary is being drawn and by whom.
2. In one paragraph, apply the concepts of belonging and nativism: explain who is included and who is written out, and who gains from drawing the boundary tightly. End with one sentence proposing a response to the exclusion you identified, and name one cost of that response.

## Sources

- OpenStax, *Introduction to Sociology 3e* (2021), the chapter on immigration and belonging.
