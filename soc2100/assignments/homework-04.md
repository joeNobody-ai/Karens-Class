# Homework 4 — A doing-gender observation

**Due:** start of Module 5 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Observe one ordinary situation this week — a meeting, a group project, a dining hall, a family gathering — and note one moment where someone was held *accountable* to a gender norm (praised, corrected, or policed for doing gender "wrong"). Describe only what you observed, no names.
2. Apply West & Zimmerman: explain how gender was being *done* in that moment — what the person was producing, and against what standard. Then name one thing your single observation cannot show (about a whole population or a general pattern).

## Sources

- Candace West & Don H. Zimmerman, "Doing Gender" (1987).
- The reference site: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/
