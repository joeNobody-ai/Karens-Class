## Student work, transcripts, and privacy

- Student submissions remain the property of the students who wrote them; nothing in this course claims
  otherwise, and instructors are reminded that they hold student work in confidence.
- Any transcript obtained while preparing to teach is kept privately and never republished. Quoted lines
  in the vocabulary reference are limited to short, credited excerpts with a link to the source, and the
  project's verifier refuses to publish a quotation that has no recorded transcript behind it.
- No student names, submissions, or assessment records are stored in this repository.

## Changes to this notice

The copyright and trademark lines appear in three places and are generated from two:

1. `LICENSE` — the repository-level notice
2. `course/rights.md` — this page
3. every page of the built site — the footer, whose text lives in `scripts/build_course.py`

To change the year, the holder, or the mark, edit the footer string in `scripts/build_course.py` and the
`LICENSE`, then rebuild. `scripts/verify_course.py` checks that the notice appears on every generated
page and that the registration symbol never appears anywhere in the built site.
