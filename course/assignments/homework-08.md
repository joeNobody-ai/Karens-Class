# Homework 8 — The criteria of judgment

**Due:** start of Session 9 · **Length:** 1.5 pages · **Weight:** counts in the homework average

## Task

A pageant is a machine for producing judgments. Your task is to take the machine apart.

1. **Extract the criteria.** From 2026 Episode 7's pageant (Miss Gilbert Hall) and original S1E17
   (*Mr. Hillman*, 1988-02-25), list every criterion of evaluation you can identify or reasonably
   infer: appearance, poise, lineage, talent, service, voice, family name. Mark which are stated in the
   material and which you are inferring.
2. **Who judges?** For each text, name who holds the power to evaluate. Then say what the judges
   themselves are accountable for.
3. **The consequence for one character.** Take Whitley in 1988: she enters to win her mother's approval.
   Write 200 words on what the pageant is *for* in her social world — not psychologically, socially.
4. **One documented consequence of colourism.** Find one citation (a peer-reviewed article or a
   research-based book chapter) documenting a measurable consequence of colourism or appearance-based
   stratification. Report author, year, the finding, and the population it was measured in.
5. **One sentence.** Does the pageant protect or discipline the people who compete in it? Answer, and
   name the evidence that decided you.
