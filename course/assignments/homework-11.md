# Homework 11 — One sentence, defended

**Due:** start of Session 12 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average

## Task

The whole assignment hangs on one sentence, and the sentence must be a claim that could be wrong.

1. **Write the sentence.** Complete it precisely: *"Season 1 of the 2026 revival argues that ______."*
   It must be a claim about an argument, not a plot summary. Test: could a reasonable viewer disagree?
   If not, rewrite it.
2. **Two scenes.** Support it with two specific moments. For each, state what it shows and what it would
   have to show instead for your sentence to be false.
3. **One counter-reading.** State the strongest reading of the season that contradicts your sentence, and
   say what evidence supports it.
4. **The verdict.** In three sentences: does your sentence survive? If you revise the sentence because of
   the counter-reading, include the revised version — revision is the assignment working, not failing.

| Points | For |
