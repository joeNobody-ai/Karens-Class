# Homework 1 — Sorting the evidence

**Due:** start of Session 2 · **Length:** 1 page · **Weight:** counts in the homework average (lowest two dropped)

## Task

1. Open the reference site's callback index: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/#callbacks
2. Choose **three** callbacks from **three different episodes**.
3. For each, answer in a compact block:
   - **What is claimed?** (one sentence, in your own words)
   - **Tier:** documented, interpretation, or instructor hypothesis. Say why.
   - **The card's own label:** what tier does the reference site give it, and do you agree?
   - **What would change your mind?** One concrete piece of evidence — something you could actually get.
4. Finish with three sentences: which of your three is the strongest claim, which is the weakest, and what distinguishes them.

## Sources

- The callbacks themselves, each with its own sources listed: https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/
- Episodes 1, 2 and 3 (2026) and the original episodes they call back to.

## Criteria (out of 10)

| Points | For |
|---|---|
| 4 | Tiers assigned correctly, with reasons that refer to the evidence, not to your feelings |
| 3 | "What would change your mind" is concrete and obtainable (not "if the writers said so") |
| 2 | Three different episodes used; claims stated in your own words |
| 1 | The closing comparison makes a real distinction rather than naming a favourite |

**Do not** quote dialogue. The published summaries are the evidence base; if you have watched the episode and want to cite a moment, describe it and mark it as *your observation*.
