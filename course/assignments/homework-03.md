# Homework 3 — An ethnography of the ritual

**Due:** start of Session 4 · **Length:** 2 pages · **Weight:** counts in the homework average

## Task

You are describing a ritual the way an ethnographer would: as a sequence of acts with participants,
rules, and consequences.

1. **Inventory the televised homecoming.** From 2026 Episode 3 (*Center de Stage*) and original S2E29
   (*A Stepping Stone*), list every ritual element you can identify: procession, performance, dress,
   food, alumni presence, who speaks, who is silent. Two columns, side by side.
2. **Who does the work?** Identify at least three kinds of labour that make each homecoming possible
   (paid, unpaid, visible, invisible).
3. **Boundaries.** Find one moment in either text when someone is placed outside the circle. State the
   rule they broke, or the attribute that excluded them.
4. **The comparison.** Write one paragraph: what does the 2026 version assume its audience already
   knows, that the 1988 version had to establish?
