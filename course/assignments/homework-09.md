# Homework 9 — Front stage, back stage

**Due:** start of Session 10 · **Length:** 1.5 pages · **Weight:** counts in the homework average

## Task

Goffman's dramaturgy, applied to a room nobody can leave.

1. **Map the geography** of 2026 Episode 8 (*I Choose Chaos*, the tornado warning) and original S5E99
   (*In the Eye of the Storm*, the hurricane). For each: who is present, what exits exist, who controls
   them.
2. **Identify front stage and back stage** for two characters in each text. Give the evidence for your
   reading — a specific behaviour that changes when the audience changes.
3. **The moment the performance stops.** Find one beat in either text where a character stops managing
   the impression they were giving. Quote the *content* of what changes, or describe the behaviour if
   you have watched it. Say precisely what made the change possible.
4. **Negotiated order.** In 200 words, argue whether the confinement *caused* the disclosure or merely
   permitted it. Use the texts as evidence and consider the alternative explanation: that the
   conversation was already overdue and something else would have triggered it.
5. **A refinement.** Write two sentences on one way the storm scenes would be different if a character
   could simply walk out.
