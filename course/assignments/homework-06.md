# Homework 6 — Who carries the memory?

**Due:** start of Session 7 · **Length:** 1.5 pages · **Weight:** counts in the homework average

## Task

1. **Build a memory table.** Choose five characters who appear in the 2026 revival and also existed in
   the original series (check the cast bridge at
   https://joenobody-ai.github.io/different-world-notes/v2/#cast). For each:
   - the character's name in both eras,
   - one specific *documented* original event they carry (season, episode, air date, what happened),
   - how the revival uses that memory — explained, implied, or ignored.
2. **The unexplained memory.** Pick the one the revival leans on hardest without explaining it. Write
   150 words on what a viewer who has not seen the original would miss, and whether the episode still
   works for them.
3. **The "aunties" as an institution.** In 2026 Episode 6, the older women arrive and take over the
   room. Describe the *mechanism* by which they hold authority — not "they are respected", but what
   actually makes the scene work that way.
4. **One sentence.** Rank the five characters by how much the revival's story depends on them, and
   justify the top choice.
