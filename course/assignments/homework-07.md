# Homework 7 — Two accounts of one category

**Due:** start of Session 8 · **Length:** 2 pages · **Weight:** counts in the homework average

## Task

2026 Episode 7 stages an argument about African and diaspora identity. Your job is to reconstruct that
argument as fairly as possible, and then adjudicate it *as evidence*.

1. **Reconstruct account A** (roughly, Kojo's position as the episode presents it) in 200 words. It must
   be an argument a reasonable person could hold, stated in its strongest form.
2. **Reconstruct account B** (the response from the American-born students) in 200 words, same standard.
3. **What is the disagreement actually about?** Choose one: ancestry, culture, nationality, class,
   or experience — and defend your choice with a specific moment or line of argument from the episode.
4. **The 1990 comparison.** In *A World Alike* (S3E60), an anti-apartheid boycott would cost a Black
   student her scholarship. Describe how that episode allocates cost, and what it assumes about its
   audience's political literacy.
5. **One paragraph of adjudication.** Which account does the revival favour, and what is your evidence?
   If the episode refuses to choose, that refusal is itself an argument — say what it does.
