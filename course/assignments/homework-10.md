# Homework 10 — Follow the money

**Due:** start of Session 11 · **Length:** 2 pages · **Weight:** counts in the homework average

This is the term's hardest homework, and the most transferable skill in the course.

## Task

1. **Find two current, citable figures** about historically Black colleges and universities. Choose from:
   - federal or state funding, appropriations, or land-grant funding gaps, **or**
   - enrolment, completion rates, or share of Black students enrolled in HBCUs.
   Use a primary source: a federal statistical agency, a federal or state education agency, a
   peer-reviewed study, or a national institution whose methodology is published.
2. **For each figure, report:** the figure; the source (agency, report/table, year); the URL; the date
   you retrieved it; the denominator or comparison group; and **one thing the figure cannot tell us**.
3. **The mechanism.** In 300 words, explain *how* the pattern your figures show is produced. Name
   mechanisms, not moral failings: appropriations formulas, duplicative programme rules, endowment
   differences, tuition dependence, state funding per student, demographic shifts.
4. **Apply it.** Return to 2026 Episode 9 and the original S2E41 (*Take This Job and Love It*). Which of
   your mechanisms is visible in each text, and which is invisible?
5. **One sentence.** State what a student movement could and could not change about Hillman's budget,
   given your mechanisms.

  confirm before you put them on a handout.
